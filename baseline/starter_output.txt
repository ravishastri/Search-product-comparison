 Here are the key shifts in the AI search market this year (2026):

**Google AI Mode is surging**
Google’s **AI Mode** is the fastest-growing major platform, rising from **9.7% of U.S. internet users in 2025 to 33% in 2026**, and is projected to overtake ChatGPT in monthly users by 2027. Meanwhile, Google’s AI Overviews now appear on **39.4% of desktop searches** (up from 25.8% a year prior).

**ChatGPT is losing prompt share**
Despite ChatGPT’s overall reach growing (est. 37.8% of U.S. internet users in 2026), it has **lost roughly 20 points of U.S. AI prompt share** to competitors like Google’s Gemini and Anthropic’s Claude.

**Business models diverged**
- **Perplexity** went fully ad-free with a subscription/credits model in **February 2026**, and revenue jumped **50%** the following month.
- **OpenAI** moved the opposite direction, testing ads in its cheapest **ChatGPT Go** tier around the same time.
- **Google** has kept ads out of AI Mode’s conversational results so far, funding it through its broader Google AI subscription bundle.

**Browser and agent battles**
The fight shifted to owning the browser itself. Perplexity launched its **Comet** browser (free worldwide since Oct 2025, iOS in March 2026), OpenAI is developing **ChatGPT Atlas**, and Google is embedding Gemini natively into Chrome—each betting that an AI agent inside the browser can handle multi-step tasks.

**Search behavior is reshaping publishing**
AI answers are significantly reducing traditional clicks. A **Pew Research study** from July 2025 found users clicked ordinary results in only **8% of visits** with an AI Overview present, versus **15%** without. Top-ranking pages saw roughly **34.5% fewer clicks** when an AI Overview appeared.

**Market shares and demographics**
- Monthly generative AI users in the U.S. are forecast at **161.5 million** in 2026.
- The user base is broadening beyond young, male demographics: female adoption is climbing, and the gender gap is expected to narrow from 12.8 percentage points in 2025 to just 7 points in 2026.
