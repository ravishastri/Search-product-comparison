## Trello Pricing and Plans

Trello offers four pricing tiers (billed per user):

| Plan | Monthly Price (Annual Billing) | Key Features |
|------|-------------------------------|--------------|
| **Free** | $0 | Up to 10 boards, unlimited cards, 250 workspace command runs/month, 10MB file attachments [https://www.spendflo.com/blog/trello-pricing-plans-costs-and-getting-the-best-deal](https://www.spendflo.com/blog/trello-pricing-plans-costs-and-getting-the-best-deal) |
| **Standard** | $5/user ($6 if billed monthly) | Unlimited boards, advanced checklists, custom fields, 1,000 automation runs/month [https://www.chanty.com/blog/trello-pricing](https://www.chanty.com/blog/trello-pricing) |
| **Premium** | $10/user ($12.50 if billed monthly) | Adds Timeline, Calendar, Table, Dashboard, and Map views; Atlassian Intelligence (AI); 14-day free trial [https://www.forbes.com/advisor/business/software/trello-pricing](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/business/software/trello-pricing) |
| **Enterprise** | From $17.50/user annually | Minimum 50 users; price per seat decreases as more users are added; includes SSO, Atlassian Guard, org-wide permissions [https://plaky.com/learn/plaky/trello-pricing](https://plaky.com/learn/plaky/trello-pricing) |

Discounts are available: non-profits receive **75% off** Standard and Premium plans, and **50% off** Enterprise; academic institutions receive **50% off** all paid plans [https://plaky.com/learn/plaky/trello-pricing](https://plaky.com/learn/plaky/trello-pricing).

## Competitive Landscape

### Asana
- **Pricing:** Starts at $10.99/user/month (free plan available) [https://blogverdict.com/blog/trello-vs-asana-vs-clickup-best-project-tool-compared](https://blogverdict.com/blog/trello-vs-asana-vs-clickup-best-project-tool-compared)
- **Comparison:** Asana offers more structured project management with task dependencies, milestones, and multiple views (List, Board, Timeline, Calendar, Gantt). Trello wins on pure simplicity and lower cost, while Asana suits teams needing structured, repeatable processes and goal alignment [https://hevodata.com/learn/asana-vs-trello-vs-monday](https://hevodata.com/learn/asana-vs-trello-vs-monday).

### Monday.com
- **Pricing:** Basic plan starts at $9/user/month (3-user minimum); Standard at $12; Pro at $24.99 [https://plaky.com/learn/plaky/trello-pricing](https://plaky.com/learn/plaky/trello-pricing)
- **Comparison:** Monday.com handles complex projects and cross-departmental workflows with more customization and 10+ project views. Trello’s free plan is more generous regarding activity logs and file storage, but Monday offers deeper automation and a more visually customizable interface [https://www.appvizer.com/magazine/operations/project-management/trello-vs-monday](https://www.appvizer.com/magazine/operations/project-management/trello-vs-monday).

### ClickUp
- **Pricing:** Free plan available; paid plans start at $7/user/month; Business at $12 [https://www.forbes.com/advisor/business/software/trello-pricing](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/business/software/trello-pricing)
- **Comparison:** ClickUp is the "everything app" with 15+ views (including Mind Maps and Whiteboards), built-in docs, integrated time tracking, and deep customization. Trello is significantly easier to learn; ClickUp has a steep learning curve and can suffer from performance issues and feature clutter [https://blogverdict.com/blog/trello-vs-asana-vs-clickup-best-project-tool-compared](https://blogverdict.com/blog/trello-vs-asana-vs-clickup-best-project-tool-compared).

### Notion
- **Positioning:** Notion combines task management with documentation and databases in an all-in-one workspace, whereas Trello focuses strictly on visual Kanban task tracking [https://www.popway.in/trello-vs-clickup-vs-notion-project-management-2026](https://www.popway.in/trello-vs-clickup-vs-notion-project-management-2026).

| Competitor | Starting Paid Price | Strength vs. Trello |
|------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Trello | $5/user/month | Simplest, easiest Kanban |
| Asana | $10.99/user/month | Structured workflows, dependencies |
| Monday.com | $9/user/month | Complex projects, visual customization |
| ClickUp | $7/user/month | All-in-one, 15+ views, built-in docs |

## Recent Reception (2024–2025)

### Ratings
- **Capterra:** 4.5/5 (13,871 reviews) [https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review](https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review)
- **Software Advice:** 4.5/5 (13,872 reviews) [https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review](https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review)
- **Apple App Store:** 4.7/5 (3,632 reviews) [https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review](https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review)
- **Google Play:** 4.6/5 (84,421 reviews) [https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review](https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review)
- **Trustpilot:** 2.2/5 ("Poor") based on 226 reviews, with recent complaints about billing cancellation difficulties and AI-only support [https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trello.com](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trello.com)

### Professional Reviews
- **SmartSuite (2026 review):** Scored Trello 9/10 for UI/usability and customer support, but only **7/10 for range of features**, noting it lacks built-in collaboration and reporting capabilities compared to alternatives [https://www.smartsuite.com/blog/trello-review](https://www.smartsuite.com/blog/trello-review).
- **Tech.co:** Ranked Trello among the best *simple* project management providers but noted its free tier is less generous than ClickUp’s, and its Premium plan offers less value than ClickUp’s comparable tier [https://tech.co/project-management-software/trello-review](https://tech.co/project-management-software/trello-review).

### User Sentiment: Pros
- Praised for an intuitive, uncluttered Kanban interface that requires virtually no training [https://theprocesshacker.com/blog/trello-review](https://theprocesshacker.com/blog/trello-review)
- Butler automation (no-code) is a standout feature for streamlining repetitive tasks [https://theprocesshacker.com/blog/trello-review](https://theprocesshacker.com/blog/trello-review)
- Strong mobile apps and good notification system [https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review](https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review)

### User Sentiment: Cons & Recent Backlash
- **Major redesign controversy (2025):** Atlassian’s Trello redesign drew harsh criticism from users, with headlines calling it potentially the "worst in tech history" and complaints that boards were repositioned as individual workspaces in a confusing layout [https://forums.theregister.com/forum/all/2025/08/06/trello_redesign_as_bad_as](https://forums.theregister.com/forum/all/2025/08/06/trello_redesign_as_bad_as).
- **Performance:** Users report interface lag when handling many tasks [https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trello.com](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/trello.com)
- **Feature limitations:** Criticized for limited reporting capabilities, lack of built-in time tracking (requires Power-Ups), and shallow subtask management compared to Asana or ClickUp [https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review](https://www.ocmsolution.com/trello-review) [https://www.smartsuite.com/blog/trello-review](https://www.smartsuite.com/blog/trello-review).

### Verdict
Trello remains the go-to choice for teams prioritizing **simplicity and speed** in visual task management. However, in 2024–2025, competitors like ClickUp and Monday.com have surpassed it in feature depth and reporting, while Trello’s recent UI redesign has frustrated some of its long-time user base. It is best suited for small teams, freelancers, and visual thinkers rather than organizations needing complex project oversight or advanced reporting.
