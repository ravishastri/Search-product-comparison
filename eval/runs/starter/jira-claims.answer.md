## Pricing & Plans

**Jira’s 2025 Cloud Tiers**
- **Free:** Up to 10 users, Scrum/Kanban boards, basic backlog management, 2 GB storage, and community-only support. (https://softwarepricingguide.com/atlassian-jira-pricing-2025-every-plan-the-data-center-vs-cloud-cost-decision-and-the-price-increases-nobody-warned-you-about)
- **Standard:** Commonly listed between **~$7.53–$8.15 per user/month** with annual billing depending on timing and team size. It removes the 10-user cap, adds audit logs, 250 GB storage, and increases automation limits. (https://ones.com/blog/jira-work-management-and-jira-software-merged-into-jira-cloud; https://softwarepricingguide.com/atlassian-jira-pricing-2025-every-plan-the-data-center-vs-cloud-cost-decision-and-the-price-increases-nobody-warned-you-about)
- **Premium:** Commonly listed between **~$13.53–$14.54 per user/month** (annual). Adds 99.9% uptime SLA, 24/7 support, advanced roadmaps, unlimited storage, and higher automation limits. (https://ones.com/blog/jira-work-management-and-jira-software-merged-into-jira-cloud; https://checkthat.ai/brands/atlassian/pricing)
- **Enterprise:** Custom annual quotes; provides 99.95% SLA and advanced analytics/security. Organizations with 801+ users may see billing around $155,000/year. (https://us.seibert.group/blog/jira-cloud-premium-vs-standard-vs-enterprise; https://checkthat.ai/brands/atlassian/pricing)

**Data Center & Hidden Costs**
- Atlassian raised Data Center list prices effective **February 11, 2025**, and increased Advantage Plan (discount) pricing by **30%** across tiers. Representative annual list prices for Jira Software Data Center include ~$42,000/year (up to 500 users) up to ~$300,000/year (unlimited users). (https://softwarepricingguide.com/atlassian-jira-pricing-2025-every-plan-the-data-center-vs-cloud-cost-decision-and-the-price-increases-nobody-warned-you-about)
- Marketplace apps and add-ons are billed per-user and can exceed Jira’s base subscription cost as teams scale. (https://www.zenhub.com/blog-posts/jira-alternatives-hidden-costs)

**Key Competitor Pricing (Starting Paid Tiers)**
- **Linear:** Basic at **$10/user/month** (annual); Business at $16. (https://www.breeze.pm/articles/compare/linear-vs-jira)
- **Asana:** Starter at **$10.99/user/month** (annual); Advanced at $24.99. (https://www.breeze.pm/articles/compare/asana-vs-jira)
- **Monday.com:** Basic from **$9/user/month**; Pro around $19. (https://atono.io/blog/most-popular-jira-alternatives-compared)
- **ClickUp:** Paid plans from **$7/user/month**. (https://atono.io/blog/most-popular-jira-alternatives-compared)
- **Azure DevOps:** Plan-based pricing; no direct per-user equivalent but competitive for existing Microsoft shops. (https://www.modernrequirements.com/blogs/top-10-jira-alternatives-for-agile-teams)

## Recent Reception (2024–2025)

**Review Aggregates**
- On SoftwareReviews, Jira holds a **composite score of 8.8/10** (CX Score 9.1/10), with recent validated reviews praising its automation rules, sandbox environment, and roadmap tracking. (https://www.softwarereviews.com/products/jira?c_id=97)
- A meta-analysis of Reddit review data showed an **overwhelming majority of users are satisfied**: 64.5% gave 5-star ratings and 34.8% gave 4-star ratings. (https://www.reddit.com/r/scrum/comments/1apv9wa/analysis_of_jira_reviews_from_popular_review)

**UX & Usability Controversies**
- Jira’s 2024–2025 unified rebranding (merging Jira Software and Work Management, renaming “issues” to “work items” and “projects” to “spaces”) generated significant backlash in the Atlassian Community. Users reported **“degraded user experience,”** distrust of the brand, and confusion around forced terminology changes. (https://community.atlassian.com/forums/discussion/3194776/feedback-about-degraded-user-experience-and-usability-of-jira-after-2025-changes)
- Some administrators criticized a new Preview Panel feature as “a complete bomb,” while others noted that non-technical users appreciated the updated navigation and inline editing in list views. (https://community.atlassian.com/forums/discussion/3194776/feedback-about-degraded-user-experience-and-usability-of-jira-after-2025-changes)
- Customer support remains a mixed bag: users find self-service resources (knowledge base, community forums) effective, but complex issue resolution is often slow for Standard plan customers who lack 24/7 dedicated support. (https://project-management.com/jira-software-review)

**Product & AI Direction**
- Atlassian continues adding AI capabilities (Atlassian Intelligence/Rovo). One beta feature, an AI-assisted JQL error fixer, has become the **most-used AI feature in Jira** by action volume. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TpISWnzN1YY)
- Jira remains the default choice for teams requiring complex workflow configuration, but competitors like Linear, Zenhub, and Shortcut are capturing dissatisfied users who prioritize speed and developer experience over configurability. (https://www.zenhub.com/blog-posts/top-jira-alternatives-for-software-teams-in-2025)
