## Pricing and plans

- Linear offers a free plan for unlimited members, capped at two teams and 250 issues. [S7][S10][S45]
- Linear's Basic plan costs $10 per user per month when billed yearly. [S7][S10][S44][S45]
- Linear's Business plan costs $16 per user per month when billed yearly. [S7][S10][S44][S45]
- Linear bills monthly or annually, with annual commitments typically offering a discount compared to month-to-month billing. [S9]
- Guest accounts require Linear's Business plan and are billed as regular members. [S45]
- Linear's AI-powered coding sessions and Loops draw on prepaid AI credits. [S45]
- Linear pricing plans are reported to range from $5,000 to $70,000 annually depending on organizational needs. [S6]

## Competitive landscape

- Jira is cited as offering advanced agile boards, over 3,000 integrations, and extensive configurability for complex, multi-department organizations. [S1][S26][S30]
- Linear is positioned as a fast, opinionated issue tracker with a keyboard-first interface and automatic sprint management for small-to-mid-size product and engineering teams. [S4][S29][S34]
- Height is identified as the closest spiritual alternative to Linear for AI-assisted issue tracking, though reports indicate its autonomous features and integrations have ended. [S2][S40]
- Linear and GitHub Issues both support coding agents and MCP servers, while Linear is noted for a clean issue activity data model, compared with GitHub Issues' timeline that is mixed with CI events, PR reviews, and deployment notifications. [S31][S32][S33]
- Linear offers a free tier scaling with usage, whereas Jira offers a free plan for up to 10 users before requiring per-seat pricing. [S26]
- Linear holds a G2 rating of 4.6 out of 5 compared to Jira's 4.3 out of 5 as of August 2026. [S29]

## Recent reception

- Linear holds a G2 rating of 4.6 out of 5 based on 122 reviews and a Capterra rating of 4.4 out of 5 based on 5 reviews. [S45]
- Linear received a Bundl editorial assessment score of 4.7 out of 5. [S44]
- Linear Agent launched in public beta on March 24, 2026, as an AI feature for triaging bugs, suggesting duplicates, creating issues from Slack, and drafting descriptions; it was free on all plans during beta. [S41][S42]
- Coding sessions that allow Linear Agent to write code using Claude Code and Codex became generally available on June 11, 2026, for Basic, Business, and Enterprise plans. [S42][S43]
- Linear is described as the default modern issue tracker for product-engineering teams that value speed, with interface transitions reported at 60 frames per second and actions responding in under 100 milliseconds. [S32][S41][S44]
- One review summarized Linear as "what happens when you start from scratch with modern UX principles," contrasting it with Jira's entrenched position in large organizations. [S28]
