## Pricing and plans

- Linear offers a free plan that supports unlimited members but limits organizations to two teams and up to 250 active issues. [S30][S33][S56]
- Linear’s Basic plan is priced at $10 per user per month and removes the 250-issue limit while allowing up to five teams. [S30][S33][S56]
- Linear’s Business plan is priced at $16 per user per month and adds support for unlimited teams, private teams, and guest access. [S30][S33][S56]
- Linear’s Enterprise plan uses custom pricing and requires annual billing. [S30][S33][S56]
- Overall Linear pricing plans can reportedly range from $5,000 to $70,000 depending on specific organizational needs. [S29] (date unknown) (price not in cited text)
- The cost of Linear is influenced by the number of teams, required features, and organization size. [S29] (date unknown)
- Linear provides a discount for annual billing. [S29] (date unknown)
