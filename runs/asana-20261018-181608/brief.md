## Competitive landscape

- Asana competes with project management platforms including Trello, monday.com, ClickUp, Wrike, and Jira. [S2][S4][S53]
- Trello manages work on boards with cards representing tasks, while Asana organizes work into projects containing subtasks, custom fields, and status tracking. [S47]
- Trello is frequently positioned as best for simple projects and small teams, whereas Asana is frequently positioned as best for enterprise teams with complex needs. [S48]
- Asana and monday.com both have a strong presence in the marketing and advertising, IT services, computer software, and non-profit organization management sectors. [S49]
- monday.com offers native document collaboration and digital whiteboards, while Asana provides document collaboration and whiteboarding through integrations. [S51]
- Asana introduced a 2026 redesign focused on AI-first navigation with embedded AI Teammates, following monday.com's 2025 interface update that included claims of faster load times. [S52]
- ClickUp offers more than 1,000 native integrations, compared with over 200 native integrations each for Asana and monday.com. [S53]
- Asana focuses on internal task management and team collaboration, while some competitors specialize in client work with built-in time tracking and budgeting. [S2] (date unknown)
