## Competitive landscape

- G2 identifies ClickUp, monday AI Work Platform, and Jira as top alternatives to Plane. [S1] (date unknown)
- The Plane blog contrasts its platform with ClickUp by describing Plane as a tighter, self-hostable workspace for engineering-led teams versus ClickUp’s broader all-in-one cloud approach. [S45]
- The Plane blog notes that Plane embeds cycles, epics, and releases in one model, offers native agent features without requiring an additional artificial intelligence purchase, and supports self-hosted deployments, unlike ClickUp. [S46]
- A customer case study on the Plane website describes how MinimalArt migrated from ClickUp to Plane to automate monthly reporting and gain real-time visibility into workloads. [S47]
- Heise Online reports that Plane is open-source project management software aimed at software developers and positioned as an alternative to Jira, Linear, Monday, and ClickUp. [S48]
- An analysis of ClickUp alternatives characterizes Plane as free and self-hostable with a modern interface covering issues, cycles, modules, and roadmaps, but cautions that its documentation and goals features are lighter than ClickUp’s and its ecosystem is newer. [S49]
- The Plane blog claims full feature parity with Jira Standard and eighty percent parity with Jira Professional, along with more than fifty thousand self-hosting teams, over one hundred thousand Docker deploys, and forty-four thousand Kubernetes deploys. [S55]
- The Plane blog also says Plane includes artificial intelligence, documentation, intake, and time tracking as native built-in features rather than afterthought plugins. [S55]
- A review of open-source project management tools calls Plane the nearest open-source equivalent to Linear and Jira Software, citing fifty-nine thousand one hundred sixteen GitHub stars and near-weekly releases. [S56]
- Heise Online notes that Plane markets itself as an open-source alternative to Jira, Linear, Monday, and Asana. [S57]
- An INNOQ review states Plane offers a clean, organized user experience that the reviewer would choose first for self-hosted agile project management, though the reviewer considers it a relatively young project compared with more mature Jira replacements. [S58]
