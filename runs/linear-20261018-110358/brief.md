## Pricing and plans

- Linear pricing plans range from $5,000 to $70,000 based on an organization's needs. [S6]
- Linear's free plan supports unlimited members but is capped at 2 teams and 250 issues. [S7][S9][S44]
- Linear's Basic plan costs $10 per user per month and its Business plan costs $16 per user per month, both billed yearly. [S7][S9][S44]
- Linear's pricing page shows yearly rates only for paid plans. [S44]
- Linear offers an Enterprise plan with custom pricing. [S9][S10]
- Annual billing commitments typically offer a discount compared to month-to-month billing. [S10]
- Guest accounts require the Business plan and are billed as regular members. [S44]
- Coding sessions and Loops require prepaid AI credits, while Linear's other AI features are included in the plan. [S44]

## Competitive landscape

- Height was previously positioned as the closest spiritual alternative to Linear with a fast, modern interface and AI-leaning autonomous features, but shut down in September 2025. [S2][S14][S30]
- Jira is positioned for larger organizations needing custom workflows, granular permissions, and compliance controls, while Linear is positioned for small and mid-size companies wanting a fast, opinionated tracker with minimal setup. [S35]
- GitHub Issues is viewed as the clear pick for open-source projects and teams that work entirely in GitHub, whereas Linear is viewed as the best issue tracker for most engineering teams. [S39]
- Linear is described as the fastest issue tracker available, featuring a keyboard-first design and fixed-length cycles, whereas Jira has a steep learning curve and its setup can take hours to days. [S25][S29][S35]
- Linear's AI features, including backlog prioritization and ticket triage integrated with coding tools, are described as more deeply embedded in engineering workflows than Jira's AI, which covers basics like summarizing and search. [S33]
- Linear offers best-in-class GitHub integration, enabling automatic issue status changes based on pull request activity and pull request review within the Linear interface. [S40][S42]
- Linear supports Web and macOS, while the now-shuttered Height also supported Windows. [S26][S30]

## Recent reception

- Linear holds a G2 rating of 4.6/5 from 122 reviews and a Capterra rating of 4.4/5 from 5 reviews. [S35][S44]
- A 2026 review describes Linear as having unmatched speed and the fastest interface of any major project management tool, with opinionated defaults that let most teams be productive within an hour. [S42]
- Linear's GitHub and GitLab integration is described as best-in-class in a 2026 review. [S42]
- Bundl.run gives Linear an editorial score of 4.7/5 as of August 2026, calling it the default modern issue tracker for product-engineering teams that value speed. [S43]
- Linear Agent launched in public beta on March 24, 2026, and Coding Sessions became generally available on June 11, 2026, on Basic, Business, and Enterprise plans using AI credits. [S41]
