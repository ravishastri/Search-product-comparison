# Launch brief: Linear

## Pricing and plans

- Linear's free plan includes "unlimited members, but capped at 2 teams and 250 issues" (S32). [S32][S35][S68]
- As of July 2026, Linear's paid tiers are priced so that "Basic runs $10 per user/month and Business runs $16 per user/month, both billed yearly" (S32). [S32][S35][S68]
- Linear offers a "custom Enterprise plan" alongside its free, Basic, and Business tiers (S32). [S32][S35]
- Linear requires that "Guest accounts need Business and are billed as regular members" (S68). [S68]
- Linear's "Coding sessions and Loops draw on prepaid AI credits, while Linear's other AI features are included in the plan" (S68). [S68]

## Competitive landscape

- Linear "earned its reputation by being the fastest, most opinionated issue tracker available. Its keyboard-first interface, automatic sprint management, and clean design make it a favorite among small-to-mid product and engineering teams" (S4). [S4]
- Linear is "primarily designed for smaller to mid-sized teams" whereas "Jira is often used by larger organizations with complex workflows and a need for extensive customization" (S52). [S52]
- Linear's application "loads instantly, transitions are animated at 60fps, and every action — creating an issue, changing status, filtering, searching — responds in under 100ms" (S56). [S56]
- Linear "is built to be navigated almost entirely without a mouse. Cmd+K command palette, quick issue creation, instant search" (S61). [S61]
- Linear offers "~40 essential dev integrations" such as GitHub, Slack, Figma, and Notion (S51). [S51]
- Linear's AI features include "Smart issue creation, prioritization, duplicate detection" (S51). [S51]
- With Linear's GitHub integration, "When the PR opens, the issue moves to 'In Review.' When the PR merges, the issue closes. The engineer never updates the tracker manually; the tracker updates itself from the work that actually happened" (S60). [S60]
- In contrast to YouTrack, "YouTrack puts in one product much of what Linear leaves to other tools: Scrum and Kanban boards, sprint planning with dependencies, time tracking, a Gantt chart" (S63). [S63]
